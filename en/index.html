TILES STUDIO
NO LIMITS FOR EXPRESSION

Custom architectural surfaces, wall panels and decorative tiles

Tiles Studio designs and produces custom ceramic tiles, three-dimensional wall coverings, large decorative panels and bespoke pieces for interiors.

We create tailored surfaces and architectural details for hotels, restaurants, bars, boutiques, retail spaces and showrooms — from feature walls and bar fronts to reception desks and room dividers.

Our work covers 3D design, prototyping, mould making and small-batch manufacturing.

We combine digital fabrication, CNC machining and 3D printing with traditional craft, using ceramics, composites, lower-impact resins, wood and metal.

Based in Barcelona, we develop one-off pieces and limited production runs for interior design, architecture and retail projects across Spain.
